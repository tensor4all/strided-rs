CPU affinity is unavailable on macOS: campaign.sh auto-detects PIN=0 and runs the
1T/4T cells unpinned (thread counts, not affinity, bound the work). The observer
was therefore not pinned either. Both campaigns passed the anchor guard with

SETTLE=5 is a macOS-only measurement fix: a 1024-atom VJP cell leaves ~1.5 GiB
being reclaimed and the next process starts ~20 % slow; a 5 s pause removes it
(measured: 16.2 -> 13.4 ms for the 1T f64 anchor). Discarded attempts are in
raw/500-discarded/ with the reason in the worklog: two runs failed the 15 % anchor
guard when the end anchor followed the 1024-atom VJP with no pause, and one ran
while a co-tenant (Koushi 60 %, UserNotificationsCore 30 %) was active.
Co-tenants were present in the accepted runs too (Koushi 40-60 % of one core
intermittently); the per-cell round-robin and the anchors are what make the
reported pair comparable. Absolute 1T numbers are the boost-clock numbers; the
4T cells are ~3.6-3.9x the 1T ones, i.e. all-core clock, not a 4x.
