(objdump -d by nm address range) + the recorded timings.

1. Kernel tier: both dtypes run V4 (AVX-512): record "simd":{"f32_lanes":16,"f64_lanes":8}.
2. Lane kernels strided_multilinear::simd::RowF32<A,B> vs RowF64<A,B> (V4), 10 shape pairs (2,1 2,2 2,3 3,1..3,7), transcript in 522-disasm.txt
   (script 522-disasm.sh; counting rule in its header: all objdump instruction lines of the symbol range, nops included; absolute counts depend on the
   build, the f32 = f64 equality does not):
   instruction counts identical per pair (e.g. <3,3>: 645 vs 645), zmm-op counts identical (106 vs 106), vcvt count 0 in both,
   hot ops 48 vmulp{s,d} + 16 vaddp{s,d} + 32 vmovup{s,d}; no FMA in either.

   0 vcvt. compile_with_block_rows<f32> has 2 plain cvt* instructions (<f64> has 0); not inspected, and where the plan is compiled relative to the timed region was not checked.
4. Cell record f32 vs f64 (cg-vjp 1T 1024): every plan field equal (nodes, edges, cg_entries, vjp_y_paths 11008, vjp_y_plan_paths 86);
   the size fields scale with element width: grad_x 2 vs 4 MiB and bar_y intermediate 368 vs 736 MiB exactly 2x; peak live 549,631,568 vs
   1,084,563,390 B = 1.97x (llmlip 1.98x, rss 1.98x). Non-width difference: timings.llmlip.alloc_calls_per_call 17 (f32) vs 46 (f64), the same
   on M5 and x86, so dtype-driven inside llmlip. Strided allocs per call = 0 in both.
5. Part speedups f64->f32 (1T 1024): vjp_x 1.96x, vjp_w 1.92x, vjp_y 1.58x, fused total 1.72x; forward 1.56x.
   llmlip f64->f32: VJP 3.63x, forward 2.26x.   (M5: strided VJP 1.78x, llmlip 1.60x.)

6. What this does and does not establish. Established: strided's f32 and f64 VJP are the same code at the instruction level for the 10 sampled lane-kernel
   shapes and the SegPolyPlan entry points, with no widening. NOT established: that those sampled shapes cover the hot path (no counters), or why the
   ratio differs between precisions. Observation, not cause: llmlip's f64 1T 1024 VJP is 4.0x slower on x86 than on M5 (1560 vs 393 ms), its f32 only
   1.75x, and llmlip allocates 17 (f32) / 46 (f64) times per call; a working-set / page-fault effect on the f64 side is consistent with this and was not
   tested (no llmlip profile).
